龍安寺

Ryōan-ji

A bed of raked white gravel, twenty-five metres by ten. Fifteen stones in five groups, set in moss.

Wherever you sit on the veranda, at least one stone is hidden behind the others. You never see the whole garden at once.

The stones are placed after the real garden's 5-2-3-2-3 grouping; positions are approximate. Their arrangement has been checked so that, as at Ryōan-ji, at least one is hidden from every point on the veranda, seated or standing. Out on the gravel, the rule no longer holds.
